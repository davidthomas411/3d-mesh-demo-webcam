Real human motion capture drives a biomechanical skeleton and one real CT scan. On the left of each clip is the original motion. On the right, the same motion applied to the rig: bone radiographs and CT reformats.

What this does and does not show

  • The CT is one scan from the open TotalSegmentator dataset. The rig is an atlas built from it, so internal anatomy is that subject's, not a patient's.
  • The CT has no arms, so arm motion shows only in the original panel; the CT panels show the shoulder girdle, spine, ribs and head.
  • Pelvis motion, root translation, fingers and face are not transferred. Spine twist and bend are constrained only through the shoulder, hip and neck keypoints.
  • The soft-tissue warp still stretches face and jaw tissue at extreme head turns. The motion is retargeted by direction fitting, not exact mocap retargeting.
  • Illustrative research output. Not patient data and not for clinical use.
